SightGuide XR — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026  |  Package: com.sightguide.xr  |  Platform: Meta Quest 3 / Meta Quest 3S

100% On-Device Offline AI Zero Camera Uploads Zero Image Storage Free Forever

1. Core Privacy Promise

SightGuide XR is a free, offline-first Mixed Reality visual assistive companion and blind-empathy experience. We collect, store, sell, and transmit ZERO personal data. All artificial intelligence models—scene description, object search, text recognition (OCR), voice recognition, speech synthesis, QR scanning, and depth safety scanning—execute 100% locally on your Quest headset.

3. Local Preferences Only

SightGuide XR stores only non-identifying preferences locally on your headset (android:allowBackup="false"): preferred language (English, Chinese, or Japanese), speech speed, voice selection, onboarding completion state, and calibrated standing eye height (a single number in meters). You can delete all local data anytime via Meta Quest Settings → Storage → SightGuide XR → Clear App Data or by uninstalling.

4. No Ads, No Accounts, No Analytics

SightGuide XR has zero ads, zero user accounts, zero cloud telemetry, and zero third-party analytics SDKs. Optional voluntary $1.00 repeatable supporter tips (supporter_tip_1usd) are processed solely by Meta Horizon Store Billing; the developer never receives payment or billing information.

Important Safety Notice: SightGuide XR is strictly an indoor assistive tool and empathy experience. It is NOT a medical device and CANNOT replace a white cane, guide dog, sighted guide, or professional mobility training. Always move carefully in safe, uncluttered indoor areas.
