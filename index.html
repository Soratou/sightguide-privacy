1. Overview & Scope

SightGuide XR is a free, offline-first Mixed Reality visual assistive companion and blind-empathy experience developed and operated by Deng Tianyi (Data Controller). All AI models—scene description, 3D object search, text recognition (OCR), voice recognition, speech synthesis, QR scanning, and hardware depth safety alerts—execute 100% locally on your Meta Quest headset with zero developer cloud servers.

Because SightGuide XR accesses official Meta Horizon Platform Services (App Entitlement Verification and optional In-App Purchases / IAP) and processes on-device headset sensor inputs, this Privacy Policy clearly explains: (a) what specific data is collected or processed, (b) how and why that data is used, (c) how long it is retained and whether it is shared, and (d) how users can request deletion of their data.

  • Local Headset Preferences (SharedPreferences, android:allowBackup="false"): Stores selected language (English, Chinese, or Japanese), speech speed, voice selection, scene description mode, tutorial completion state, calibrated standing eye height (a single numeric value in meters), and the local count of completed voluntary supporter tips. Stored exclusively on your headset to remember your setup between sessions.
  • Voluntary Support Correspondence: If you email dty6652909@gmail.com for support, feedback, or privacy inquiries, we collect your email address and message content solely to respond to and resolve your request.

3. Data Sharing, Third Parties & Retention

  • No Third-Party Data Processors or Sales: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third-party advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or external data processors.
  • No Ads & No Tracking SDKs: SightGuide XR contains zero advertisements, zero user-account logins, and zero third-party telemetry or crash-tracking SDKs.
  • Platform Billing Provider: Official Meta Horizon Store entitlement checks and voluntary IAP transactions interact directly with Meta Platforms, Inc. via the Meta Horizon Platform SDK.
  • Retention: Ephemeral camera, depth, hand-tracking, and voice data are retained for 0 seconds after local inference. Local on-device preferences persist only until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Support emails are retained only as long as necessary to address your inquiry or until you request deletion.

4. How to Request Deletion of Your Data

You have the right to request deletion of any data associated with your use of SightGuide XR at any time:

  1. Email Data Deletion Request (Developer Records & Support Emails): Send an email to dty6652909@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - SightGuide XR. Please include the email address you contacted us from (and, if inquiring about a voluntary supporter tip, your Meta display name or purchase/order ID). Because SightGuide XR operates zero external backend servers, we hold no cloud profiles; we will permanently delete any developer-held emails or records and confirm completion in writing within 7 business days (and always within 30 calendar days).
  2. Instant On-Device Deletion (Local Preferences & Local Tip Count): You can immediately and permanently delete all data stored locally by SightGuide XR on your headset by opening Meta Quest Settings → Storage → SightGuide XR → Clear App Data, or by uninstalling SightGuide XR.
  3. Meta Horizon Store Account & IAP Transaction Records: Underlying Meta Horizon Store account and payment records are hosted and managed by Meta Platforms, Inc. You may view, manage, or request deletion of your Meta platform and purchase records directly via the Meta Accounts Center.

SightGuide XR is self-certified for Teens and Adults (ages 13 and older, or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, please contact dty6652909@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

Important Safety Notice: SightGuide XR is strictly an indoor visual assistive companion and blind-empathy experience. It is NOT a medical device and CANNOT replace a white cane, guide dog, sighted guide, or professional mobility training. Always walk carefully in safe, uncluttered indoor spaces and avoid holding your hands in front of the headset depth sensors while walking.
